Find the Right Solution

The essential equipment, explained.

Tank size

Larger tanks are more forgiving. A 60-litre tank or bigger is an easier start than a tiny bowl.

Filter

Moves water and holds the bacteria that clean it. Choose one rated for your tank size.

Heater and thermometer

Tropical fish need steady warmth. A thermostat heater keeps the temperature from swinging.

Test kit and conditioner

A liquid test kit shows ammonia, nitrite and nitrate. Conditioner makes tap water safe.

Quality You Can Trust

Water quality is the heart of fishkeeping.

  • Fish waste becomes ammonia

    Ammonia is toxic. Helpful bacteria turn it into nitrite, then into the far less harmful nitrate.

  • Cycling takes time

    Most new tanks need around four to six weeks before the bacteria can handle a full fish load.

  • Water changes remove nitrate

    Changing a quarter of the water each week is a common routine for small community tanks.

  • Overfeeding is a quiet danger

    Give only what the fish finish in about two minutes. Extra food rots and fouls the water.

Get Started Today

Set up your first tank.

  1. Place and fill

    Put the tank on a level, sturdy stand away from direct sun, add rinsed substrate and fill with treated water.

  2. Run the filter and heater

    Switch on and let the tank run while it cycles.

  3. Test regularly

    When ammonia and nitrite read zero and nitrate appears, the tank has cycled.

  4. Add fish slowly

    Start with a few hardy fish, then wait a couple of weeks before adding more.

Questions people ask

Can I put fish in right after filling the tank?

It is safer to cycle first. Adding many fish to an uncycled tank often causes ammonia spikes.

How many fish can I keep?

Fewer than you think. Start light and add slowly, watching your test results.

Do all fish need a heater?

No. Tropical fish do, while some, like white cloud mountain minnows, prefer cooler water.

Is a goldfish bowl a good idea?

No. Goldfish grow large and produce a lot of waste, so they need a big tank and a strong filter.
